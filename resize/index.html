Exam Photo

Resize a photo or signature for an exam form

Choose the exam, choose your picture, and get a JPG of the right pixels and KB. It all happens in your browser: the picture is not uploaded.

or drop one here

Drag the picture to move it. Use the slider to zoom. What you see inside the frame is what you get.

How it works

  1. Exam: pick yours and the tool sets the pixels and the KB range from that exam's notice. Or choose "My own size" and type them.
  2. Picture: choose a photo from your phone or computer. On a phone you can also take one with the camera.
  3. Frame: move and zoom until the face or the signature fills the frame nicely.
  4. Make my file: the tool saves a JPG and adjusts the quality until the file is inside the KB range.

Good to know

  • "Make the paper white" helps signatures, thumb impressions and handwritten declarations photographed in poor light. Leave it off for face photos.
  • If a picture is very small, the file can come out below the minimum KB. The tool then adds blank filler inside the file to reach the minimum. The picture does not change.
  • A KB range is counted carefully: at least the minimum in units of 1,024 bytes and at most the maximum in units of 1,000 bytes, so the file passes whichever way a form counts.
  • The tool cannot judge your photo. Background, lighting, cap or glasses rules are on each exam page.

Check the official notice before you upload. We read these rules in the documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. A new notice can change them, and the form on the day is the final word. This site is not connected with any exam body.
