CUET PG 2026 photo and signature size

For CUET PG 2026 the scanned photograph must be between 10 and 200 KB and the scanned signature between 10 and 50 KB, both clearly legible.

Covers: CUET (PG) 2026. Source: 2026 Information Bulletin. Checked on 11 November 2026.

Resize my signature for CUET PG

Graduates often reuse an old photo. Check it first.

Many CUET PG applicants pick a photo that is already on the phone from a college form. Open it and look at three things before using it: is it recent, is the background plain and light, and is the face large and sharp. Then check the file size. A photo straight from a phone camera is usually 2,000 KB or more, far above the 200 KB limit, so it must be made smaller.

Making the signature fit in 50 KB

Fifty KB is small for a phone photo but generous for a signature. Crop tightly around the signature so that little blank paper is left, and the file will come down easily while the lines stay sharp. Our resizer makes a 420 by 180 pixel image and adjusts the quality until the file lands inside 10 to 50 KB.

The bulletin gives no pixel size, so the pixel sizes used by the tool are our choice.
