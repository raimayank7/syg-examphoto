CTET photo and signature size (September 2026 bulletin)

CTET gives both a file size and a paper size. The photo is 10 to 100 KB and 3.5 cm wide by 4.5 cm high. The signature is 3 to 30 KB and 3.5 cm long by 1.5 cm high. Both are JPG or JPEG.

Covers: CTET September 2026. Source: September 2026 Information Bulletin. Checked on 11 November 2026.

What the notice says, upload by upload

Photograph

  • Scanned photograph in JPG or JPEG format, between 10 KB and 100 KB.
  • Image dimension 3.5 cm (width) by 4.5 cm (height).
  • It should be your latest photograph.

Centimetres on a screen: what that means

A file has pixels, not centimetres. A size in centimetres only becomes pixels once you choose how many dots per inch to use. At 300 dots per inch, 3.5 cm by 4.5 cm is 413 by 531 pixels, and 3.5 cm by 1.5 cm is 413 by 177 pixels. Our resizer uses these, so the shape matches the bulletin exactly and the picture stays sharp. The 300 figure is our choice; the bulletin does not name one.

CTET has the smallest photo limit of the big exams

The CTET photo limit of 100 KB is half of what NTA allows for JEE Main, NEET and CUET (200 KB). A photo prepared for those forms may be too large here. The signature can go as low as 3 KB, which is rare; most forms start at 10 KB.

Keep both files ready before you start

The bulletin advises candidates to keep the scanned images of the latest photograph and signature ready, in the stated size and dimension, before applying online. Uploading both is mandatory.

Questions people ask

What is the CTET photo size?

Between 10 KB and 100 KB in JPG or JPEG format, with an image dimension of 3.5 cm (width) by 4.5 cm (height).

What is the CTET signature size?

Between 3 KB and 30 KB in JPG or JPEG format, with an image dimension of 3.5 cm (length) by 1.5 cm (height).

How many pixels is 3.5 cm by 4.5 cm?

It depends on the dots per inch you choose. At 300 dots per inch it is 413 by 531 pixels. At 200 dots per inch it is 276 by 354 pixels.
