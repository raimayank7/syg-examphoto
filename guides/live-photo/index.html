Live photo capture in exam forms: how to get it right

More and more forms open your camera and take the photo themselves. You cannot fix a live photo with an editing tool afterwards, so the preparation happens before you press the button.

Who asks for it

  • SSC: the live photo is the only photo. No photo file is uploaded.
  • UPSC: you upload a photo and also capture a live one while filling the Common Application Form.
  • IBPS and SBI: in addition to the uploaded photograph, a live photograph is captured by webcam or mobile phone.
  • NTA (JEE Main, NEET, CUET): a live photograph is uploaded at the time of filling the form.

Why exam bodies do this

SSC's notice gives the reason plainly: there have been cases of people trying to impersonate candidates, and a photo taken live while the form is filled ties the application to the person. Staff at the exam venue compare your face with that photo.

Set up before you start the form

  1. Light: face a window or lamp. Avoid a bright window behind you.
  2. Background: a plain, light wall. NTA asks for white.
  3. Camera height: at eye level. Put a laptop on a few books, or prop the phone upright.
  4. Face: remove cap, mask and sunglasses. For SSC, remove spectacles too.
  5. Distance: fit your whole face inside the outline the form shows, neither too close nor too far.
  6. Stillness: rest your elbows or the phone on something, so the picture is not shaken.

No webcam?

NTA and IBPS let you scan a QR code shown on the screen with a phone, and take the picture on the phone instead. Keep the phone's camera lens clean and use the same light and background rules.

Never photograph a photograph

Holding a printed passport photo in front of the camera is the one thing SSC says will get the application rejected. The live photo must be of you, there and then.

Look the same on exam day

SSC's notice says there should be no change in appearance between the photo and the exam day. If you plan a big change, such as shaving a beard, do it before you fill the form, not after.

Check the official notice before you upload. We read these rules in the documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. A new notice can change them, and the form on the day is the final word. This site is not connected with any exam body.
