CUET UG 2026 photo and signature size

CUET UG 2026 needs a photo of 10 to 200 KB and a signature of 10 to 50 KB, both JPG or JPEG. The signature limit is smaller than in JEE Main and NEET, so a file made for those may be too big here.

Covers: CUET (UG) 2026. Source: 2026 Information Bulletin. Checked on 11 November 2026.

What the notice says, upload by upload

Photograph

  • A recent passport size photo, in colour, against a white background.
  • The face should fill about 80% of the picture, with both ears visible and no mask.
  • Between 10 KB and 200 KB, in JPG or JPEG format, clearly legible.
  • A live photograph is also uploaded while filling the form.

Resize my photograph for CUET UG

Signature

  • JPG or JPEG format, clearly legible, between 10 KB and 50 KB.

Resize my signature for CUET UG

Applying to more than one NTA exam?

NTA runs JEE Main, NEET and CUET, and the photo limit is the same in all three bulletins we read: 10 to 200 KB. The signature limit is not the same. It is 10 to 100 KB in JEE Main 2027 and NEET UG 2026, but 10 to 50 KB in CUET UG 2026.

A signature file between 10 and 50 KB works for all three. If you make one file for every form, make it to the CUET limit.

No pixel size is given

The bulletin asks for a recent passport size photograph but gives no pixel or centimetre size for the photo or the signature. Our resizer makes a 413 by 531 pixel photo and a 420 by 180 pixel signature. Those pixel sizes are our choice.

Identity proof image

Candidates who verify their identity by a method other than Aadhaar or DigiLocker upload a scanned identity proof as a JPG or JPEG of 10 to 200 KB. A disability or UDID certificate, where needed, is a PDF of 50 to 300 KB.

Questions people ask

What is the CUET UG 2026 photo size?

Between 10 KB and 200 KB, in JPG or JPEG format.

What is the CUET UG 2026 signature size?

Between 10 KB and 50 KB, in JPG or JPEG format, clearly legible.

Can I use my JEE Main signature file for CUET?

Only if it is 50 KB or smaller. JEE Main 2027 allows up to 100 KB, but CUET UG 2026 allows up to 50 KB.
