NEET UG 2026 photo, signature and thumb impression size

NEET UG 2026 asks for a passport size photo of 10 to 200 KB, a signature of 10 to 100 KB, and impressions of the fingers and thumbs of both hands. You also need a printed postcard size photo on exam day.

Covers: NEET (UG) 2026. Source: 2026 Information Bulletin. Checked on 11 November 2026.

What the notice says, upload by upload

Passport size photograph

  • A recent passport size photograph, in colour or black and white, against a white background.
  • The face without a mask, including ears, should fill 80% of the image.
  • JPG or JPEG, clearly legible, between 10 KB and 200 KB.
  • Spectacles are allowed only if you use them regularly. Polaroid and computer-generated photos are not acceptable.
  • You also upload a live photograph while filling the form.

Signature

  • JPG or JPEG format, between 10 KB and 100 KB.

Fingers and thumb impressions (both hands)

  • Left and right hand fingers and thumb impressions, in JPG or JPEG.
  • The bulletin gives two ranges in two places: 10 KB to 200 KB in the list of specifications, and 50 KB to 300 KB in the photograph instructions.
  • A file between 50 KB and 200 KB fits both, so that is what our resizer makes.
  • Unclear impressions lead to rejection.

Two photos: one to upload, one to carry

The upload is the passport size photo. Separately, the bulletin asks candidates to paste one postcard size (4 inch by 6 inch) colour photograph with a white background on the proforma downloaded with the admit card, and to bring it to the centre. It reminds candidates to keep 6 to 8 passport size and 4 to 6 postcard size photographs.

The bulletin also says the same passport size photo is to be used for the online form and for pasting on the attendance sheet at the centre. So get prints made from the same picture you upload.

The finger and thumb impressions

Put the impressions of your left and right hand fingers and thumbs on white paper, photograph or scan the sheet, and save it as a JPG. Because the bulletin shows two different size ranges, aim for the overlap, 50 to 200 KB. If the form on the day shows its own limit next to the upload button, follow the form.

What the bulletin says will be rejected

  • Applications without photographs, or with unclear photographs.
  • Photographs that look fabricated, de-shaped, hand-made or computer made.
  • Unclear finger and thumb impressions.

The bulletin also notes that correction of the photograph, signature and impressions will not be given, so upload the right files the first time.

Certificates

Category certificate, Class 10 certificate and marks-sheet, address proof and similar papers go up as PDF files of 50 to 300 KB. They are documents, so use a PDF tool for those, not an image resizer.

Questions people ask

What is the NEET UG 2026 photo size in KB?

Between 10 KB and 200 KB, in JPG or JPEG format, for the passport size photograph.

What is the NEET UG 2026 signature size?

A JPG or JPEG file between 10 KB and 100 KB.

What is the postcard size photo for NEET?

A 4 inch by 6 inch colour photograph with a white background. It is not uploaded. It is pasted on the proforma downloaded with the admit card and carried to the examination centre.

Can the NEET photo be black and white?

The 2026 bulletin says the passport size photograph should be either in colour or black and white. The postcard size photograph for exam day must be in colour.
