IBPS photo, signature, thumb impression and declaration size

IBPS asks for four pictures: your photograph, signature, left thumb impression and a handwritten declaration. Each has its own pixel size and KB range, and the form will not move ahead until they fit.

Covers: PO/MT, Specialist Officers and RRBs (Officers and Office Assistants). Source: CRP XVI and CRP RRBs XV notifications. Checked on 11 November 2026.

What the notice says, upload by upload

Photograph

  • A recent passport style colour picture, 4.5 cm by 3.5 cm.
  • Light coloured, preferably white, background. Look straight at the camera with a relaxed face.
  • Dimensions 200 x 230 pixels (preferred). File size between 20 KB and 50 KB.
  • No caps, hats or dark glasses. Religious headwear is allowed but must not cover the face. With glasses, no reflections, and the eyes must be clearly seen.
  • In addition, you capture a live photograph by webcam or mobile phone during registration.

Signature

  • Sign on white paper with a black ink pen.
  • Dimensions 140 x 60 pixels (preferred). File size between 10 KB and 20 KB.
  • A signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is not accepted.
  • If the signature on the attendance sheet or call letter does not match, the applicant will be disqualified.

Left thumb impression

  • Put your left thumb impression on white paper with black or blue ink.
  • Dimensions 240 x 240 pixels in 200 DPI, that is 3 cm by 3 cm. File size 20 KB to 50 KB.
  • Without a left thumb, the notification lists what to use instead (right thumb, then fingers), and asks you to name the finger used in the uploaded document.

Handwritten declaration

  • Write the declaration in English, clearly, on white paper with black ink, in your own handwriting.
  • Dimensions 800 x 400 pixels in 200 DPI, that is 10 cm by 5 cm. File size 50 KB to 100 KB.
  • Not in CAPITAL LETTERS. If it is written by somebody else or in another language, the application is treated as invalid.

The declaration text

The notification gives the exact words to write by hand:

I, _______ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required.

Write your own name in the blank. Use ordinary handwriting, not capital letters. The notification says a candidate who cannot write may get the text typed and put the left thumb impression below it.

How IBPS says to scan

  • Set the scanner to at least 200 dpi and to True Colour.
  • Crop to the edge of the photograph, signature, thumb impression or declaration.
  • Save as JPG or JPEG.
  • If a file is larger than allowed, the notification suggests lowering the scanner settings such as dpi and number of colours.

A phone camera works too. Take the picture from straight above in daylight, then crop and resize it with the tool on this site.

Things that get an IBPS form stuck or rejected

  • Photo and signature swapped: each must go in its own place. The notification says a candidate who gets this wrong will not be allowed to appear for the exam.
  • An unclear or smudged face, signature, thumb impression or declaration.
  • Signature or declaration in capital letters.
  • A small photo, a shadow on the face, sunglasses or a cap in the captured photo.

Is IBPS Clerk the same?

We read the annexure in three notifications (PO/MT-XVI, SPL-XVI and RRBs XV) and the sizes are identical in all three. We have not read the Customer Service Associate (Clerk) notification for this cycle ourselves, so check its Annexure before you upload.

Questions people ask

What is the IBPS PO photo size?

Dimensions 200 x 230 pixels (preferred) and a file size between 20 KB and 50 KB, in JPG or JPEG. The paper size named is 4.5 cm by 3.5 cm.

What is the IBPS signature size?

Dimensions 140 x 60 pixels (preferred) and a file size between 10 KB and 20 KB. Sign on white paper with a black ink pen, not in capital letters.

What is the size of the left thumb impression?

240 x 240 pixels in 200 DPI, which is 3 cm by 3 cm, with a file size of 20 KB to 50 KB, in JPG or JPEG.

What is the size of the handwritten declaration?

800 x 400 pixels in 200 DPI, which is 10 cm by 5 cm, with a file size of 50 KB to 100 KB, in JPG or JPEG.

Can I type the IBPS declaration?

No, it must be in your own handwriting, in English. The notification allows one exception: candidates who cannot write may get the text typed and put their left thumb impression below it.
