How to use this site

  1. Open your exam's page and read what its notice asks for.
  2. Tap the resize button on that page. The tool opens with the right size already set.
  3. Choose your picture, move and zoom it inside the frame, and tap Make my file.
  4. Check the result, download it, and upload it in the exam form.

Three things the notices agree on

  • Format: every exam here wants JPG or JPEG. Not PNG, not PDF, not HEIC from an iPhone.
  • Clarity beats size: every notice warns that unclear or blurred pictures can get an application rejected.
  • Live photos are spreading: SSC, UPSC, IBPS, SBI and NTA now also take a photo through your camera while you fill the form.

Questions people ask

Is the resizer free, and is my photo uploaded anywhere?

It is free and needs no login. The picture is resized inside your own browser. It is not uploaded to this site or to anyone else.

Where do the sizes on this site come from?

From the official notice, information bulletin or website of each exam body. Each exam page names the document and the date we read it. We do not copy sizes from other websites.

Why do other sites show different numbers?

Exam bodies change the rules from year to year, and many pages keep showing an older year. For example, SSC now takes the photo live instead of asking for a file. Always look at the date and the source.

What if my file is below the minimum KB?

The resizer first raises the picture quality. If a small picture is still below the minimum, it adds blank filler inside the file so that it reaches the minimum size. The picture itself does not change.

My exam is not listed. Can I still use the tool?

Yes. Open the resize tool, choose "My own size" and type the pixels and the KB range from your notice.
