About Exam Photo

Exam Photo helps you get the photo, signature and other pictures for an Indian exam form to the right size, and explains the rule for each exam in plain words.

Where the rules come from

Every size on this site was read by us in the official notice, information bulletin or website of the exam body. We do not copy figures from coaching sites or other tools, because those often show an older year. Each exam page lists its documents under Sources. All were checked on 11 November 2026.

  • SSC: Notice of Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026 (paras 9.4 to 9.6); Notice of Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination, 2026 (para 9.6); Notice of Constable (GD) Examination, 2026 (para 8.7); Notice of Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs Examination, 2026; Notice of Stenographer Grade C and D Examination, 2026 (para 9.9)
  • UPSC: Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE dated 04.02.2026, Civil Services Examination (Notes 2 and 3); Examination Notice No. 08/2026-CAPF, Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination; FAQ of the earlier UPSC One Time Registration portal (question on photograph and signature size)
  • IBPS: Detailed Notification CRP PO/MT-XVI, Annexure III: Guidelines for Scanning and Upload of Documents; Detailed Notification CRP SPL-XVI (Specialist Officers), same annexure; CRP RRBs XV notification (revised), same annexure
  • SBI PO: Recruitment of Probationary Officers, Advertisement No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09, section on scanning and upload of documents
  • JEE Main: Information Bulletin, Joint Entrance Examination (Main) 2027, National Testing Agency (Step 2: Application Form)
  • NEET UG: Information Bulletin for NEET (UG) 2026, National Testing Agency (upload specifications and photograph instructions)
  • CUET UG: Information Bulletin, CUET (UG) 2026, National Testing Agency (application form: photograph and signature)
  • CUET PG: Information Bulletin, CUET (PG) 2026, National Testing Agency (instructions on scanned photograph and signature)
  • CTET: Information Bulletin, CTET September 2026, Central Board of Secondary Education (instructions for uploading scanned images)
  • GATE: GATE 2027, Photograph and Signature page, Indian Institute of Technology Madras

Where a notice gives a size in centimetres or gives no pixel size, the pixel size our resizer makes is our own choice, and the page says so.

Exams we have not added

We add an exam only after reading its own notice. Railway (RRB) notices could not be opened from their official sites on the day we checked, so RRB is not listed yet.

Your pictures stay with you

The resizer works inside your browser. Your photo and signature are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Not an official site

This site is independent. It is not connected with SSC, UPSC, IBPS, SBI, NTA, CBSE, IIT Madras or any other organisation. The official notice and the form itself always decide.
