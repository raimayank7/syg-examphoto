JEE Main 2027 photo and signature size

For JEE Main 2027 the photo must be a JPG or JPEG of 10 to 200 KB and the signature a JPG or JPEG of 10 to 100 KB. You also take a live photo while filling the form.

Covers: JEE Main 2027. Source: 2027 Information Bulletin. Checked on 11 November 2026.

What the notice says, upload by upload

Photograph

  • A recent passport size photo, in colour, against a white background.
  • The face should fill about 80% of the picture, with both ears visible and no mask.
  • Between 10 KB and 200 KB, in JPG or JPEG format, clearly legible.
  • You also upload a live photograph at the time of filling the form, using a webcam or by scanning a QR code with a phone.

Signature

  • JPG or JPEG format, clearly legible, between 10 KB and 100 KB.

Numbers that changed from older years

Many pages still show 10 to 300 KB for the photo or 4 to 30 KB for the signature. Those are from earlier bulletins. The 2027 bulletin says 10 to 200 KB for the photograph and 10 to 100 KB for the signature.

No pixel size is given

The bulletin does not give a size in pixels or centimetres for either image. It asks for a passport size photograph. Our resizer makes the photo 413 by 531 pixels, which is the common 3.5 cm by 4.5 cm passport shape, and the signature 420 by 180 pixels. Both pixel sizes are our choice; the KB limits are the bulletin's.

The live photo

The bulletin says to switch on your camera or connect a webcam. If the device has no camera, you can scan a QR code with a phone or tablet and take the picture there. It asks for a white background, proper lighting and 80% face visibility including ears, without a mask.

Other files in the same step

The Class 10 certificate or marks-sheet goes up as a PDF of 50 to 300 KB. The same PDF limit applies to a disability or UDID certificate where it is needed. These are documents, not pictures, so this site's image resizer is not meant for them.

Your own photo, uploaded correctly

The bulletin warns that the photograph and signature must be the candidate's own, and that corrections are possible only in the correction window NTA announces. Check both previews before you submit.

Questions people ask

What is the JEE Main 2027 photo size in KB?

Between 10 KB and 200 KB, in JPG or JPEG format, as stated in the JEE (Main) 2027 Information Bulletin.

What is the JEE Main 2027 signature size?

A JPG or JPEG file between 10 KB and 100 KB, clearly legible.

What background should the JEE Main photo have?

White. The bulletin asks for a recent passport size photograph in colour with 80% face, including ears, without a mask, against a white background.
